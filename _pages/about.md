Mabuhay! 👋 
  
I'm a PhD student at [MBZUAI](https://mbzuai.ac.ae) supervised by [Dr. Alham Fikri Aji](https://afaji.github.io) working on novel approaches to **Modeling Multilinguality**. In particular, my work centers on methods that [reframe multilinguality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10310) in an efficient and linguistically-motivated manner, beyond simply cramming hundreds of unique languages within the same billion-ish parameters.

Besides this, I've worked on various other topics under the multilinguality and low-resource umbrella, including:
* **Code Switching** -- Multilingual speakers naturally [code-switch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Code-switching) in two or more languages when speaking to peers, but multilingual models are still lacking in capabilities to [understand](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.774/) and [execute](https://aclanthology.org/2023.calcs-1.5/) this phenomenon.
* **Resources & Evaluation** -- More data is often the best remedy to "very little data". Beyond [Filipino resources](https://huggingface.co/jcblaise) and [benchmarks](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.127/) for my home region, I have also collaborated for Southeast Asian [tools](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10118) and [resources](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.916/) as a co-founder of [SEACrowd](https://seacrowd.org/) and [ACL SIGSEA](https://www.sigsea.org/). 
* **Beyond Southeast Asia** -- Beyond my home region, I've also worked on global efforts for [cultural QA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.05967), [cultural MT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.24456) and [large-scale cultural benchmarking](https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-long.167/). More recenty, I've dabbled in topics such as [theory of mind](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.1230/) and [competitive programming](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.11332), leveraging my past as an ICPC contestant.
* **Applications in Low-resource** -- Lastly, I've worked on improving performance in tasks such as [Multilingual Translation](https://aclanthology.org/2021.wmt-1.52/), [Question Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.01107), [Fake News Detection](https://aclanthology.org/2020.lrec-1.316/), and more -- all constrained under low-resource settings.

Prior to my PhD, I was Lead Research Engineer at [Samsung Research](https://research.samsung.com) where I worked on low-resource machine translation and dialogue generation. I've also previously been affiliated with [Mila - Quebec AI Institute](https://mila.quebec/en) and [McGill University](https://mcgill-nlp.github.io/), the [University of the Philippines](https://eee.upd.edu.ph/), [De La Salle University](https://www.dlsu.edu.ph/research/research-centers/adric/celt/), and [Senti AI](https://senti.ai).

If you're interested in collaborating or if you want to chat about low-resource languages, feel free to get in touch! You may reach me through my email `me (at) blaisecruz (dot) com`.
