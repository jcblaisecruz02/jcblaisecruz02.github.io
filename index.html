Mabuhay!

I'm a PhD student at MBZUAI supervised by Dr. Alham Fikri Aji, working at the intersection of lexical semantics and multilinguality. My thesis centers on multilingual models that anchor on word senses as canonical cross-lingual representations .

My broader interests span translation, low-resource languages, benchmarking, and code-switching. These interests led me to co-found SEACrowd and ACL SIGSEA with close collaborators in Southeast Asian NLP.

Prior to my PhD, I was Lead Research Engineer at Samsung Research where I headed teams working on dialogue systems used by 5M+ customers worldwide. Further previous affiliations include Mila, McGill University, and more.

  2. May 29, 2026

    Previously, sense representations demanded specialized architectures and from-scratch training. We solve this by showing that sense representations are inducible properties of any pretrained model.

    My new paper, Sense Representations are Inducible Interfaces is now out on arXiv as a preprint.

  3. Jan 16, 2026

    Current multilingual models cram 100+ languages into one parameter space and call the result multilingual. We argue for a linguistically grounded alternative: model multilinguality as sense adaptation, where new languages anchor onto representations of meanings the model already knows.

    My new paper, Multilinguality as Sense Adaptation, is now out as a preprint on arXiv.

    Special thanks to Mila - Quebec AI Institute and McGill University for hosting me while working on this project!
