Happy to announce the release of our new paper, [HyperBrowseComp](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03574){:.paper-title}! We've worked hard to produce a really challenging multilingual and multimodal stress test for agentic systems, and we're excited to see how your models deal with what we've set up.

Check out the [project site](https://hyperbrowsecomp.github.io/) as well for a quick overview and a sample of the questions.
