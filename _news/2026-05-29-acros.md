---
title: Inducing Sense Representations on Pretrained Models
date: 2026-05-29
---

Previously, sense representations demanded specialized architectures and from-scratch training. We solve this by showing that sense representations are inducible properties of any pretrained model.

My new paper, [Sense Representations are Inducible Interfaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28669){:.paper-title} is now out on arXiv as a preprint.
