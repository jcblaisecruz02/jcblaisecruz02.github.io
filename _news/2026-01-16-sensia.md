---
title: Multilinguality as Sense Adaptation
date: 2026-01-16
---

Current multilingual models cram 100+ languages into one parameter space and call the result multilingual. We argue for a linguistically grounded alternative: model multilinguality as sense adaptation, where new languages anchor onto representations of meanings the model already knows.

My new paper, [Multilinguality as Sense Adaptation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10310){:.paper-title}, is now out as a preprint on arXiv.
