title: Anchor on word senses
---

My ACROS paper introduces a mechanism to **induce sense representations in any pretrained decoder**, unlocking capabilities that previously required from-scratch training with specialized architectures such as WSD, lexical steering, and sense-based cross-lingual adaptation via SENSIA.
