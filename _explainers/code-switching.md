title: Code switching
---

Multilingual speakers naturally [code-switch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Code-switching) in two or more languages when speaking to peers, but multilingual models are still lacking in capabilities to [understand](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.774/) and [execute](https://aclanthology.org/2023.calcs-1.5/) this phenomenon.
