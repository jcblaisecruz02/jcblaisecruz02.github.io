title: Low-resource languages
---

More data is often the best remedy to "very little data". Beyond [Filipino resources](https://huggingface.co/jcblaise) and [benchmarks](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.127/) for my home region, I have also collaborated on Southeast Asian [tools](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10118), [models](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.916.pdf), and [resources](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.916/).

I've also worked on improving performance in tasks such as [Multilingual Translation](https://aclanthology.org/2021.wmt-1.52/), [Question Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.01107), [Fake News Detection](https://aclanthology.org/2020.lrec-1.316/), and more -- all constrained under low-resource settings.
